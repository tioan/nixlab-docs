# Lab map

Every machine and every service of the [inventory](inventory.md), by site: at the top each site's line with its networks, below it the hosts with the services they run, at the bottom the services bought in, by provider. A click on a service or a host draws what it depends on, what it uses, where it is backed up to and what it is reached through, and shows its entry next to the map; the names there can be clicked again. On the right of a service, one tag per endpoint tells how it is reached: `pub` public, `prox` through a proxy, `mesh` mesh only, `lan` LAN only; a click on one selects the service with that endpoint. A click on an empty spot of the map enlarges it.
